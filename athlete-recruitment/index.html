What the program includes

Five deliverables, produced and maintained for you.

Each of these can be taken on its own, and they work best together: the résumé and the profile are what a coach reads, the video is what a coach watches, and the monthly management is what keeps both current.

description

Professional Academic & Athletic Résumé

Grades, transcripts, playing history, positions, teams, tournaments and achievements organised into one document a university or a coach can read in minutes.

public

Digital Athlete Profile Hosting

A hosted profile that gathers the academic record, the football history and the video into a single link, ready to send to a coach or an admissions office.

movie

Professional Soccer Highlight Video Editing

Your footage cut into a short, professional reel built around what college coaches actually watch: first touch, decisions under pressure and movement off the ball.

school

College Recruitment Guidance

Guidance through the process itself: which programs fit the athlete's level and grades, how and when to approach coaches, and what a realistic timeline looks like.

update

Monthly Profile Management

Résumé, statistics and highlights updated as the season progresses, so the profile a coach opens is never a year out of date.
