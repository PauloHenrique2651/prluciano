About Eternal Eleven

Development built on football, education and personal growth

Eternal Eleven Agency works with committed young players and the families behind them. Every athlete is developed across five pillars, with a plan that builds technical skill, tactical intelligence and mental resilience, and with schoolwork treated as part of the program rather than a distraction from it. The work happens in training weeks, in international experiences and in the academic record universities ask to see.

Our development model

Five pillars. One complete athlete.

Every athlete we work with develops across the same five foundations, and they are trained together rather than in isolation. A player who is strong in one and weak in another eventually stalls at the next level.

fitness_center

Physical

Speed, agility, coordination, mobility and recovery. The physical base that keeps technique intact once the game speeds up.

sports_soccer

Technical

Ball control, footwork, first touch, passing and finishing, drilled until they hold under pressure.

strategy

Tactical

Positioning, anticipation, game awareness and decision-making. Learning to read the game before the ball arrives.

psychology

Psychological

Confidence, focus, motivation and emotional control. The mental work that separates players of equal ability.

groups

Human

Discipline, respect, responsibility, teamwork and education. The character that makes a career, and a life, sustainable.

LE11 – Specialized Player Development Program

Train with Purpose. Develop Your Skills. Elevate Your Game.

LE11 is expanding its training programs to offer specialized sessions for players in every position on the field. Our professional training sessions focus on developing the technical, tactical, physical, and mental skills required to compete at a higher level. We offer private and small-group training sessions throughout the week, open to LE11 program members and outside players, led by Luciano Emilio, former MLS MVP and Golden Boot winner.

Defenders

Defensive positioning, anticipation, tackling, marking, and decision-making.

Midfielders

Ball control, passing accuracy, vision, movement, and transitions.

Strikers

Finishing techniques, attacking movement, positioning, and goal-scoring instincts.

Inside the professional game

A look inside a professional club environment

Participants have the opportunity to visit D.C. United training facilities, observe first-team training and, when schedules and club authorization allow, meet players. Access is arranged around the club's calendar and approvals, so what each group experiences is confirmed in advance. This is a genuine look inside a professional environment: it is not a guaranteed partnership.

Education & future

The athlete who is also a student keeps more doors open

We treat academic commitment as part of the training program. Players keep their grades at a level that preserves their options, and families get help organizing the record universities actually ask for: transcripts, results, playing history, positions, teams, tournaments and achievements. None of it promises a scholarship or admission. It makes the athlete a credible candidate, and then the athlete earns the rest.

Pathway to Professional Soccer

From youth development to professional opportunities.

Through our partnership with Dictionary Academy, Eternal Eleven Agency has established a successful pathway for talented young players to advance to D.C. United Academy.

This collaboration has helped numerous youth players transition from developmental academies to one of the leading professional soccer academies in the United States. Some players have even progressed to make their first-team debuts with D.C. United in Major League Soccer (MLS).

Our proven track record reflects our commitment to player development, talent identification, and creating opportunities for young athletes to reach the highest levels of professional soccer.

Why Eternal Eleven

What families are actually choosing

What separates a development program is not what it promises but how it works week to week. These are the six things we hold ourselves to, and the things we are happy to be judged on.

Experience

Led by a program director with a professional career across several countries, an MLS MVP season and coaching experience inside the US system.

Personalized development

Plans are built around the individual athlete: their position, their gaps and the level they are aiming for, not a fixed group schedule for everyone.

Holistic approach

Physical, technical, tactical, psychological and human development are trained as one system rather than five separate agendas.
